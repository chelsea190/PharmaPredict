What do these numbers mean?
  • MAE: average daily error, in units.
  • RMSE: like MAE, but big misses count more.
  • WAPE: total daily error as a share of units sold. 30% means off by 30% of volume.
  • Totals WAPE: error on each medicine's 7, 14 and 30 day totals. This is what you order against, and it is how the model is chosen.
  • Bias: above zero means over-forecasting (overstock risk); below zero means under-forecasting (stock-out risk).
